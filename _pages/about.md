We are Tongji MRL, a student team focused on autonomous surface vehicles, perception, planning, and system integration for maritime robotics competitions.

Our current work focuses on platform integration, autonomous navigation, perception in complex water environments, and preparation for RobotX 2026.

This site is used to share our team updates, technical progress, and development milestones throughout the RobotX 2026 journey.

As the project develops, we will continue to add team members, publications, outreach activities, and competition highlights here.
